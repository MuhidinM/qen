Add Ethiopian public holidays with `getHolidays(year)`, `getHolidaysInRange(start, end)` and `nextHoliday(from)`, filterable by category (public by default). Includes the fixed-date holidays: Enkutatash, Meskel, Genna, Timket, Adwa, Labour Day, Patriots' Victory and the Downfall of the Derg.
