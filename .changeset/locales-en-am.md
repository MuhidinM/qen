Add the `Locale` type and English and Amharic locales as separate entry points: `@qen/core/locales/en` and `@qen/core/locales/am`. Each has month names for all three calendars, weekday names, era labels and Ethiopian time period names. The Amharic locale is marked `reviewed: false` until a native speaker has checked it.
