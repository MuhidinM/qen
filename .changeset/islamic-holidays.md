Add the Islamic holidays: Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha and Mawlid (public), plus the Islamic New Year, Ashura, Isra and Mi'raj, the start of Ramadan, Laylat al-Qadr (approximate) and the Day of Arafah. They follow the Hijri method, can fall twice in one Gregorian year, and are marked `status: 'expected'`. `getHolidays` now returns 13 public holidays a year.
