# Arithmetic and comparison

Month and year arithmetic happens in the date's own calendar (13 months per Ethiopian year) and clamps to the last day of the month.

<LiveExample
  title="Adding days, months and years"
  code={`
import { addDays, addMonths, addYears, date } from '@qen/core';

show(addDays(date('ethiopian', 2018, 13, 5), 1));
show(addYears(date('ethiopian', 2015, 13, 6), 1));
show(addMonths(date('gregorian', 2025, 1, 31), 1));
`}
/>

`diff` counts months and years the same way, in the calendar of its first argument. `age` gives years, months and days; a birthday that doesn't exist in a year (Pagume 6, 29 February, a Hijri 30th) is reached on the month's last day.

<LiveExample
  title="Differences and ages"
  code={`
import { age, date, diff } from '@qen/core';

show(diff(date('ethiopian', 2018, 11, 30), date('ethiopian', 2019, 1, 1), 'months'));
show(age(date('ethiopian', 1990, 1, 17), date('ethiopian', 2019, 2, 7)));
`}
/>

Dates in different calendars compare by the day they fall on:

<LiveExample
  title="Comparing across calendars"
  code={`
import { compare, date, isSame } from '@qen/core';

show(isSame(date('ethiopian', 2019, 2, 7), date('gregorian', 2026, 10, 17)));
show(compare(date('ethiopian', 2019, 1, 1), date('gregorian', 2026, 9, 12)));
`}
/>
